How big is a cord of wood?

A full cord is 128 cubic feet of stacked wood: 4 ft high, 4 ft deep, 8 ft long.

TermCubic feetStacked asWhat to watch
Full cord1284×4×8 ftThe only measure defined in law in most US states.
Face cord / rick~434×8 ft, one 16" row deepRoughly a third of a cord, and the most common way people overpay.
Half cord644×4×4 ftFine, as long as the price is half.
Thrown / loose cord~180 loosetossed, not stackedLoose wood holds more air. It settles to well under a stacked cord.
Pickup bed (8 ft)~65-70level, not heapedA half-ton truck cannot legally carry a full cord of oak, that is roughly two tons.

The face-cord trap

A "face cord" has a fixed height and length but no fixed depth, so it means whatever the seller's saw length was. At 16 inch pieces it is about a third of a real cord. If a price is quoted per face cord, ask the piece length before comparing it to anything: three sellers can quote the same number for genuinely different piles.
