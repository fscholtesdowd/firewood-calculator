The 4 plans

Each one below is a real U.S. EPA Burn Wise Program, "Build Firewood Storage" publication (document numbers included), not a reprint of someone else’s blog plan. All four use cheap, common materials - landscape timbers, cinder blocks or pavers, and either pallets, a tarp, or plywood for the roof.

1/3 Cord, T-Post Ends

Holds: 1/3 cord (about 42.7 cu ft) — Extendable to hold more wood.

Base(2) 8-foot landscape timbers
Ends(4) 6-foot metal T-posts
Roof(1) 8'x24" galvanized metal roof panel
ToolsHammer, safety glasses, gloves

Tips from the plan:

  • Slope roof to prevent water collection
  • Use a large enough roof so the overhang protects wood from rain & snow
  • Stack larger pieces of firewood at the base to increase stability
  • Season wood at least 6 months for soft wood and 12 months for hard wood

EPA doc EPA-456/F-22-010 · full plan (PDF)

1/3 Cord, 2"x4" Ends

Holds: 1/3 cord (about 42.7 cu ft) — Extendable to hold more wood.

Base(2) 8-foot landscape timbers, (2) cinder blocks
Ends(2) 2"x4"x10' treated lumber (cut in half)
Roof(1) 8'x10' tarp
ToolsGloves, safety glasses, saw

Tips from the plan:

  • Slope roof to prevent water collection
  • Use a large enough roof so the overhang protects wood from rain & snow
  • Stack larger pieces of firewood at the base to increase stability
  • Extend base using 2 additional landscape timbers + 1 cinder block

EPA doc EPA-456/F-22-09 · full plan (PDF)

1/2 Cord

Holds: 1/2 cord (about 64 cu ft) — Fixed frame, biggest single-pallet-base design.

Base(4) pavers or bricks, (1) pallet
Ends(2) 2"x4"x10' treated lumber (cut in half)
Roof(1) sheet of 4'x8' treated plywood
ToolsGloves, safety glasses, saw

Tips from the plan:

  • Use a large enough roof so the overhang protects wood from rain & snow
  • Stack larger pieces of firewood at the base to increase stability
  • Season wood at least 6 months for soft wood, and 12 months for hard wood
  • Depending on pallet type, may need to use 6-foot metal T-posts as ends

EPA doc EPA-456/F-22-08 · full plan (PDF)

Full Cord

Holds: 1 cord (about 128 cu ft) — Largest design, built from two pallets end to end.

Base(4) cinder blocks, (2) pallets, (2) 8-foot landscape timbers
Ends(2) 2"x4"x6' treated lumber, (2) pallets
Roof(1) 10'x12' tarp
ToolsGloves

Tips from the plan:

  • Leave some space between the stacks to allow air to circulate
  • Pallets are often available for free at retail shops and hardware stores
  • Slide treated lumber into cinder block to support upright pallets
  • Use a large enough roof so the overhang protects wood from rain & snow

EPA doc EPA-456/F-22-07 · full plan (PDF)

Rules that apply to all four

  • Protect your wood from the elements by extending the shed roof 1 foot on all sides.
  • Use a base to keep wood off the ground to reduce pests and moisture.
  • Slope roof to prevent water collection.
  • Leave some space between the stacks to allow air to circulate.

Before you stack it full

Season wood at least 6 months for soft wood, and 12 months for hard wood. A rack keeps wood off the ground and the top dry; it does not season wood faster by itself. See how long your species needs and how to tell it is actually ready before you rely on a full rack for this winter.

Source: U.S. EPA Burn Wise Program, "Build Firewood Storage" (https://www.epa.gov/burnwise/storage), 4 flyers EPA-456/F-22-07 through -010, fetched 2026-09-20. See the source page.
