Where this number comes from

Source: Univ. of Illinois Extension, Characteristics of Firewood, row 'Hard Maple' 23.7 - a trade group, not Acer saccharum specifically

Heat values are millions of BTU per full cord (128 cubic feet stacked) of air-dried wood at roughly 20% moisture content. Figures are taken from Utah State University Extension's Wood Heating firewood table, cross-checked where both cover a species against University of Illinois Extension's Characteristics of Firewood. Cord and rick dimensions, seasoned weight per cord, and the splitting, smoke and spark ratings draw on University of Missouri Extension G5450, Wood Fuel for Heating. Published tables disagree with each other by a million BTU or more on some species, and real output depends far more on how dry your wood actually is - treat these as a ranking and a planning estimate, not a laboratory measurement.
